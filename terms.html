Demian is an AI spiritual companion grounded in Demianist doctrine. It runs on large language models reached through an AI gateway. It is software, not a person, and it never claims otherwise: it will confirm that it is an AI at any point in a conversation, however the question is put.

Demian is not a substitute for professional help. It does not provide medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal, financial, or tax advice, and nothing it says should be treated as any of those. If you are dealing with something that needs a doctor, a therapist, a lawyer, or an accountant, see one — Demian may be useful alongside that, never in place of it.

Demian speaks from a specific doctrine, and does not pretend to be neutral. Demianism is a revealed doctrine with its own scripture, axioms, and a vision that extends to society and civilization, and some of its positions sit against the dominant assumptions of contemporary culture. Demian is built to reason from that doctrine honestly rather than to agree with you. You may find its positions wrong, and you are free to say so, to disagree with it, and to stop using it. AI output is not guaranteed to be correct. Language models can be wrong, can misunderstand, and can state things confidently that are untrue. Immediate delivery and the 14-day right of withdrawal

Under EU consumer law you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a contract concluded online, without giving a reason. There is an exception for digital content and digital services delivered immediately, and it is directly relevant here, so it is spelled out rather than buried.

Everything sold here is delivered at once: a mantra session begins the moment you return from checkout, and credit is available to spend as soon as it lands. Before you pay, you are asked to confirm two things — that you expressly request immediate performance, and that you acknowledge that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal once performance has begun. That confirmation is a condition of proceeding to payment, and it is recorded.

The practical effect: once a mantra session has started, or once purchased credit has been used, the 14-day right no longer applies to that purchase. This is not a waiver of your rights invented here — it is Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU, and it only holds because you were asked first and told plainly what you were agreeing to.

Where the right does still apply. If you bought credit and have not spent any of it, performance has not begun and you keep the full 14-day right of withdrawal — write to demian@demianism.org within 14 days of purchase and the payment will be refunded in full, no reason needed. The same applies to a mantra you paid for but never began.

Bonus credit is granted as part of a purchase and has no independent cash value. Where a refund is due on a purchase that carried a bonus, the refund is of the amount you actually paid.
