Who’s brave enough to jump aboard this ghost ship rising from the mists? It will truly take a leap of faith from someone special.

Isle of the Immortals, Barabeke, 2026. Also on the cover of Ritorno a Eden (2nd book).

The base of the Demianist community will be this public forum, where we can debate Demianism, propose ideas, and get to know each other. Anyone is welcome to participate, including those who want to criticize Demianism.

I will take part to the forum actively, since I believe there is a lot I need to answer for. I want to do it publicly, with Demianist honesty. Ask and it shall be given to you.

Demianists will be recognized by the label “Demianist,” which in practice indicates those who believe in the 13 axioms. Anyone who identifies as a “demianista” should tell me, and I, for the sake of equity and inclusion, will assign them the title without questioning their self-identification with the risk of causing them micro-traumas 😄

Let us meet here, then, and discuss Demianism. I have brought it for you. It is up to each of you to become its interpreters. I will try to serve as enabler, inspirer, and initial guardian and ferryman. My aim is to leave behind an autonomous organization that stands on its own, democratically, one that won't need the rule of a charismatic leader. The leader is the axioms.

My aim is to plant the seed of a new culture, one that is capable of rising into a system, a renewed foundation for the individual, the family, the community, and the nation — one that can survive the centuries and protect us from decadent, oppressive, and dehumanizing drifts.
